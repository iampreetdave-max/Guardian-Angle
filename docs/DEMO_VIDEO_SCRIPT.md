# Demo Video — Shot List + Guj/English Anchor Lines (5:00 max)

You'll freestyle the narration (Gujarati + English mix — natural for a Gujarat
Police audience). This doc gives you: (1) the **shot list with key points you
must land per scene**, and (2) optional **anchor lines** in Gujarati written in
English characters — use them as-is or riff around them. Tech terms stay in
English; that's how it sounds natural anyway.

Timing rule of thumb: if a scene runs long, cut words — never cut the clicks.

---

## Before you hit record (15 minutes, in order)

1. **Pre-index the fire clip** — upload a `test_clips/fire/` clip to a camera
   feed **10–15 min before recording** (CPU indexing takes 30–90 s, and a fresh
   anomaly alert then shows "minutes ago"). Verify it appears in **Live Alerts**
   and its **auto-created case** exists in Cases.
2. Sign in as `admin@city.gov / admin123`. Stay admin throughout.
3. Open **City Map** once and pan around — caches OSM tiles.
4. In **CrimeGPT**, select case #1 and confirm **Suggest Sections** returns
   results; leave the tab on Data Pool.
5. Browser 1920×1080, 100% zoom, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off.
   Recorder: 1080p / 30 fps, mic on.
6. One silent click-through first. Record **scene by scene** and cut — no
   single perfect take needed.

## If something misbehaves while recording

- **Search thin results** → small demo index; line still works. Re-record after
  indexing one more clip.
- **Routes don't draw** → re-click Plan; worst case narrate over the Patrol
  plan side panel text.
- **PDF slow** → keep a pre-generated chargesheet PDF on the desktop, cut to it.
- **Anything else** → `python scripts/demo_reset.py` from `backend/`
  (15 self-checks), re-record that scene only.

## After recording

Upload (YouTube unlisted / Drive) → send the link → it goes into slide 10 of
the PPT with a QR (`python backend/scripts/gen_ppt.py`).
