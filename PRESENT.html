If they push on the numbers

“How accurate is the search?”
84.6% macro recall, 61.5% top-1, zero false positives — measured on 16 real HD clips with ground truth checked frame by frame. Face re-identification returns the source frame at rank 1.
“How good is the prediction?”
Hit-Rate@10 0.79, PAI@10 2.37× against a 2.53× oracle ceiling — 94% of what any predictor could achieve on this data. We publish the ceiling on purpose. It is synthetic data and we say so on the slide.
“Which parts of the model actually matter?”
We ran an ablation. Only recency decay is statistically significant. The anomaly boost contributes exactly zero to the backtest — deliberately, because feeding present-time anomalies into a historical fold would be leakage. It is in docs/ABLATION.md.
“Isn’t this just off-the-shelf models?”
Yes — CLIP, YOLOv8, ArcFace, FAISS, ByteTrack, and we trained none of them. The contribution is the closed loop and the offline CPU constraint: camera anomaly → auto case → risk bump → nearest-unit dispatch, on hardware a station already owns. docs/PRIOR_ART.md has 35 citations and states what we did not solve.
“Does tracking work on fast traffic?”
Not well, and the app says so itself. Keyframes are ~2 fps, so a highway car clears its own length between frames. The quality badge is graded by measured fragmentation: market good, highway degraded, junction poor. We would rather show our worst camera than hide it.
